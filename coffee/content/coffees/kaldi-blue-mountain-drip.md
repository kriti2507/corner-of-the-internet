Picked up a Café Kaldi Drip bag of the Blue Mountain Blend.

## What it is

A single-serve drip bag (10 g, one cup) of Kaldi's Blue Mountain Blend. Kaldi describes it as a blend with Blue Mountain in it, with a "dignified" taste where the aroma, sweetness, bitterness and acidity are well balanced.

- **Beans:** Colombia, Jamaica, Brazil and others. Kaldi doesn't say how much of the blend is Blue Mountain.
- **Roast:** the drip bag doesn't say. Kaldi sells the same blend as 200 g beans and calls that a medium roast.
- **Price:** ¥220 a bag, or ¥1,100 for five, on Kaldi's online store (November 2026). The 200 g beans are ¥2,786.
- **Where:** Kaldi shops and Kaldi's online store.
