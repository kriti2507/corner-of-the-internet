Went to the Starbucks in Ueno Park today and had the Joyful Medley tea. The tea, not the latte: I picked it because it's the lower-calorie one.

## What it is

Joyful Medley is Starbucks Japan's holiday tea. Starbucks describes it as a black tea blend with oolong for sweetness, jasmine for a floral smell and a faint apricot note.

- **Calories:** about 2 kcal for a Tall of the plain tea, against about 186 kcal for the Joyful Medley tea latte with regular milk.
- **Price:** a Tall is ¥510 to stay or ¥501 to take away (2026).
- **When:** the holiday menu, from late October 2026 until it sells out.
