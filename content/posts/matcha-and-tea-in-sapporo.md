Sapporo is a coffee city first, but there's good matcha and tea if you know where to look. These are the places I actually went to while I lived there. Lines in quotes are my own notes from Google Maps. The rest is what each place is known for and what other visitors tend to say. Each name links to a short page with the details.

## For serious matcha

- **[d:matcha Kyoto Sapporo](/places/d-matcha-kyoto-sapporo)**, Sapporo Station. A Kyoto tea farm's own shop. Unsweetened lattes, single-origin matcha, and tastings with the owner. Book ahead.
- **[Gyokusuien](/places/gyokusuien)**, Sōsei East. A family tea shop since 1933. Get the matcha soft serve, with a full bowl of matcha poured on top. Closed Sundays.

## For a long sit with tea

- **[Johnson's Tea Lounge](/places/johnsons-tea-lounge)**, Odori. A Japanese tea café upstairs in a furniture store, with a socket at every seat and cheap refills.
- **[MaShu](/places/mashu-jingu-no-mori)**, Maruyama. Herbal tea and desserts, looking out at the woods by Maruyama Park. Closed Mondays.

## On the way somewhere

- **[Natural Life Style sabi](/places/natural-life-style-sabi)**, Minami-ku. "Nice curry and matcha." A camping shop and café on the road to Jōzankei.
- **[THE ALLEY, Daimaru Sapporo](/places/the-alley-daimaru-sapporo)**, Sapporo Station. Bubble tea in the Daimaru basement, for a quick sweet drink while shopping.

## A matcha latte at a coffee place

- **[Standard Coffee Lab.](/places/standard-coffee-lab)**, Tanukikoji. "White chocolate matcha latte with beautiful latte art." More in [the coffee guide](/blog/best-coffee-in-sapporo).
