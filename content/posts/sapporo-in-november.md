November is the quiet month before winter. The first snow that settles in the city usually comes around mid-November, though it often melts again before winter sets in. Days are short and grey, but hotels are cheap and there are no crowds.

## What's on

- **Sapporo Chrysanthemum Festival.** In the underground walkways, in early November (2026: 1–3 November).
- **Sapporo White Illumination.** Lights along Odori Park and the city's main streets. In 2026 it opened on 20 November. 2026 is its 46th year.
- **Munich Christmas Market in Sapporo.** German food, mulled wine and Christmas gifts at Odori Park 2-chome, from 20 November to 25 December 2026.
- **The giant Christmas tree at Sapporo Factory** goes up in early November.

## What to do

Have a warm drink at the Christmas market, then walk the lights. It's a good month for long [coffee](/blog/best-coffee-in-sapporo) and [matcha](/blog/matcha-and-tea-in-sapporo) stops, and for [onsen](/blog/onsen-in-and-around-sapporo). The ginkgo avenue at Hokkaido University often holds its colour into early November.

## Around Hokkaido

Ski areas near Sapporo, like Sapporo Kokusai, Sapporo Teine and Kiroro, usually open in late November. Most mountain roads and hiking areas close for the winter.

## Good to know

Switch to winter shoes as soon as the snow starts. The first icy weeks catch everyone out.
