July is high summer, and it's why many people from the rest of Japan escape to Hokkaido. Days are warm, with highs mostly in the mid-20s °C, and it's far less humid than Tokyo, though recent summers have had some hotter days.

## What's on

- **Sapporo Summer Festival and the Odori Beer Garden.** Odori Park turns into one long beer garden, with Japan's big four breweries, a world beer area and a German village. In 2026 it ran from 23 July to 18 August.
- **Pacific Music Festival (PMF).** An international classical music festival, mostly at Kitara concert hall and the Sapporo Art Park. In 2026 it ran from 7 to 27 July.
- **Makomanai fireworks.** Fireworks set to music in early to mid-July.
- **Sapporo Craft Beer Forest.** A craft beer festival at the Bankei ski area in early to mid-July.
- **Sapporo City Jazz Park Jazz Live.** Free jazz on Odori in mid-July.

## What to do

Spend an evening at the beer gardens, then walk to Susukino. During the day, head to the hills or the coast. The [outdoors guide](/blog/sapporo-outdoors-and-day-trips) has easy ideas.

## Around Hokkaido

Lavender in Furano peaks in mid to late July, and Farm Tomita is the classic place to see it. See [Furano, Biei & Tomamu](/blog/hokkaido-furano-biei-tomamu). Shakotan's sea urchin season is in full swing. See [Otaru, Shakotan & Niseko](/blog/hokkaido-otaru-shakotan-niseko).

## Good to know

July and August are peak season for Hokkaido road trips, and rental cars sell out. Book early.
