June is early summer in Sapporo, and it's a great time to visit. Hokkaido doesn't really get the rainy season that soaks the rest of Japan, so days are mostly fresh and dry. Average highs are around 22°C, with long, light evenings.

## What's on

- **YOSAKOI Soran Festival.** Hundreds of dance teams perform across Odori Park and the city, mixing Kōchi's yosakoi dance with the Hokkaido folk song Sōran-bushi. In 2026 it ran from 10 to 14 June, and in 2027 it's from 9 to 13 June.
- **Hokkaido Shrine Festival (Sapporo Matsuri).** Every year from 14 to 16 June, with a procession through the city and stalls around Nakajima Park.
- **Sapporo Flower Carpet.** Flower designs laid out on Akapla, the brick-paved plaza near the old government building, in mid to late June.
- **Jōzankei Natsutōro.** Evening lanterns light up the onsen town from June to October.

## What to do

Eat outdoors, watch the dancing, and stay out late while it's light. It's a good month for the [outdoors guide](/blog/sapporo-outdoors-and-day-trips) and for a day trip to the coast.

## Around Hokkaido

June to August is sea urchin season on the Shakotan Peninsula, and the sea there is famously blue. See [Otaru, Shakotan & Niseko](/blog/hokkaido-otaru-shakotan-niseko). Rebun's flowers peak in June and July. See [Wakkanai, Rishiri & Rebun](/blog/hokkaido-wakkanai-rishiri-rebun). The first lavender appears in Furano at the end of the month.

## Good to know

YOSAKOI week gets busy around Odori, and roads close for the parades. Book hotels early for that week.
