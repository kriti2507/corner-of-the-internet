Asahikawa is Hokkaido's second-biggest city, about 1 hour 25 minutes from Sapporo by limited express. It's the gateway to Daisetsuzan, the biggest national park in Japan.

## Asahiyama Zoo

The zoo is famous for showing animals as they move in the wild: seals swimming up a vertical tube, penguins swimming overhead. In winter, the king penguins go for a walk through the snow. The walk usually runs from late December to mid-March, at 11am and 2:30pm (only at 11am from March).

## Asahikawa ramen

A soy-sauce ramen with a layer of fat on top to keep it hot through the winter. There are ramen shops all over the city.

## Daisetsuzan

I've been to the **Daisetsuzan** mountains, the roof of Hokkaido. The Asahidake ropeway takes you up to alpine meadows and steaming vents, and Sōunkyō gorge has an onsen town under tall cliffs. The colours here turn first in Japan, usually from mid-September.

## On the way: Hokuryū

The **Hokuryū Sunflower Village**, between Sapporo and Asahikawa, has fields of sunflowers in midsummer. I've been there too.

## Good to know

Daisetsuzan is real mountain country. Weather turns quickly, and bears live here. If you hike, go prepared.
