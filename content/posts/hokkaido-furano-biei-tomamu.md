This is the Hokkaido from the postcards: patchwork fields, lavender, and the Tokachi mountains behind. It's 2 to 3 hours from Sapporo, and best done with a car over one or two nights.

## Furano

**[Farm Tomita](/places/farm-tomita)** is the classic lavender farm. Lavender starts in late June and peaks in mid to late July, and the farm grows other flowers into autumn. In summer, a seasonal train, the Furano Lavender Express, runs from Sapporo.

## Biei

Rolling farmland, lone trees and the **[Shirogane Blue Pond](/places/shirogane-blue-pond)**, a pond of strangely blue water in the woods. The fields are working farms, not parks. Biei has been dealing with overtourism, so stay on the roads and never walk into the fields for a photo.

Up the road from Shirogane, **[Fukiage Onsen](/places/fukiage-onsen)** is a free, natural outdoor bath in the mountains below Tokachidake.

## Tomamu

A resort in the mountains. These are the places I went to there.

- **[Unkai Terrace](/places/unkai-terrace-tomamu).** A gondola takes you up at dawn to watch a sea of clouds, when the weather lines up. It's open from mid-May to mid-October (in 2026, from 13 May to 13 October), roughly 4:30 to 8am.
- **Chapel on the Water.** Tadao Ando's chapel, with a cross standing in a pond.
- **Ice Village.** In winter, a small village of ice domes with an ice bar and an ice chapel. Last season it ran from 10 December to 14 March.

Further north, I've also stopped at **Sandandaki Park** in Ashibetsu, a waterfall park on the road between Sapporo and Furano.

## Good to know

Lavender season is the busiest time of year here, and car parks fill early on weekends. Go on a weekday morning.
