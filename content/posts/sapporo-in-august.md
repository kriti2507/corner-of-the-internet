August is the warmest month in Sapporo, with highs in the mid-20s °C and warm nights. By the end of the month, the evenings start to feel like autumn.

## What's on

- **Odori Beer Garden** until mid-August (in 2026, until 18 August).
- **Susukino Festival.** The nightlife district closes its streets for food stalls, a courtesan parade (oiran dōchū) and portable shrines. In 2026 it ran from 6 to 8 August.
- **Hokkai Bon Odori.** Bon dancing to live folk music at Odori Park 2-chome, in the middle of August (2026: 13–16 August). Anyone can join.
- **Rising Sun Rock Festival.** An all-night outdoor rock festival at Ishikari Bay New Port, in mid-August.
- **Hokkaido Marathon.** It starts and finishes at Odori Park in late August.

## What to do

Join the Bon dancing, even if you don't know the steps. On hot days, escape to the mountains or the sea. [Jōzankei](/places/jozankei-bridge) is an hour away, and the Shakotan coast makes a full day out.

## Around Hokkaido

August is the last month of sea urchin season on the Shakotan Peninsula. See [Otaru, Shakotan & Niseko](/blog/hokkaido-otaru-shakotan-niseko). The sunflowers at Hokuryū bloom in midsummer, on the way to Asahikawa. See [Asahikawa & Daisetsuzan](/blog/hokkaido-asahikawa-daisetsuzan).

## Good to know

Obon, in mid-August, is a big travel week in Japan, and trains and flights fill up.
