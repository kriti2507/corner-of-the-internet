Sapporo's mountains start right at the edge of the city, so you can be on a trail half an hour after leaving the centre. These are the parks, walks and viewpoints I actually went to while I lived there. Each name links to a short page with the details.

## Short hikes from the city

- **[Sankakuyama](/places/sankakuyama-trail)**, Yamanote. A forest trail to a small summit with a view over the whole city, about 30 minutes up. Bring a bear bell.
- **[Heiwa Falls](/places/heiwa-falls)**, Nishi-ku. A small waterfall and shrine at a Mt Teine trailhead. A nice place to cool off after the climb.

## Parks

- **[Tenjinyama Ryokuchi](/places/tenjinyama-ryokuchi)**, Toyohira. A quiet hill park with wisteria in early summer, cherry blossoms in spring and maples in autumn.
- **[Shiroi Koibito Park](/places/shiroi-koibito-park)**, Miyanosawa. The Shiroi Koibito cookie factory, with free gardens and a paid factory tour. Touristy, but fun, and good on a rainy day.

## A day out to Jōzankei

- **[Jōzankei Bridge](/places/jozankei-bridge)**. The classic view of the hot-spring town's gorge, about an hour from Sapporo by bus. Best in autumn.
- **[Sapporo Lake Observation Deck](/places/sapporo-lake-observation-deck)**. A free lookout over the lake and dam above Jōzankei. You need a car.
- On the way, stop at **[Natural Life Style sabi](/places/natural-life-style-sabi)** for curry and matcha.

When you're done, warm up at [an onsen](/blog/onsen-in-and-around-sapporo), or start the day with [good coffee](/blog/best-coffee-in-sapporo) or [matcha](/blog/matcha-and-tea-in-sapporo).
