Wakkanai is the northernmost city in Japan, about 5 hours 10 minutes from Sapporo on the Sōya limited express. From there, ferries cross to Rebun Island (about 2 hours) and Rishiri Island.

## Rebun, the island of flowers

Rebun is called the floating island of flowers. More than 300 kinds of alpine plants bloom there at sea level, from late May to September, peaking in June and July. Some grow nowhere else, like the Rebun lady's slipper orchid. The Momoiwa trail from the ferry port is the classic walk, with flowers and views of Mt Rishiri.

Places I've been to on Rebun:

- **[Cape Sukoton](/places/cape-sukoton).** The island's northern tip. My note: "Good cape."
- **Cape Sukai,** just south of it, with its bright blue water.
- **The seal watching spot.** My note: "Seals."
- **Midorigaoka Park campsite.** My note: "Camping place, good facility."

## Rishiri

A round island that's basically one mountain, Mt Rishiri, often called Rishiri Fuji. It's famous for kombu seaweed and sea urchin in summer.

## Good to know

Go between June and August. Outside that, many places close, and ferries can be cancelled in rough weather. Bring a windproof jacket even in summer.
