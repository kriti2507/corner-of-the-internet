I lived in Sapporo before moving to Tokyo in 2026, and the city changes completely through the year. Winter is long and snowy. Summer is short, dry and mild. Spring and autumn come and go fast. This page is the short version, and each month has its own page with the events, the weather and what to do.

Event dates move a little every year, so check the official sites before you book. Where the next dates are already announced, the month pages list them.

## The short answer

- **For snow and festivals:** early February, for the [Snow Festival](/blog/sapporo-in-february). Book a hotel months ahead.
- **For flowers and easy weather:** late May to June. Lilacs, YOSAKOI, long days, no rainy season.
- **For summer:** July. Beer gardens on Odori, lavender in Furano, and it's cooler than the rest of Japan.
- **For food:** September, for the Autumn Fest on Odori.
- **For autumn colour:** October. Jōzankei first, then the ginkgo trees at Hokkaido University.
- **For quiet and cheap:** November, or early April once the snow has gone.

## Month by month

- **[January](/blog/sapporo-in-january):** the coldest month. Illuminations, skiing, and drift ice arriving in the east.
- **[February](/blog/sapporo-in-february):** the Snow Festival, Otaru's Snow Light Path and the ice festival at Lake Shikotsu.
- **[March](/blog/sapporo-in-march):** the melt. Slushy streets, late-season skiing, fewer people.
- **[April](/blog/sapporo-in-april):** the snow goes, carp streamers fly in Jōzankei, and cherry blossoms start at the end of the month.
- **[May](/blog/sapporo-in-may):** cherry blossoms and Golden Week, then the Lilac Festival.
- **[June](/blog/sapporo-in-june):** YOSAKOI Soran, the Hokkaido Shrine Festival, and sea urchin season.
- **[July](/blog/sapporo-in-july):** beer gardens, fireworks, music festivals and lavender.
- **[August](/blog/sapporo-in-august):** Susukino Festival, Bon dancing on Odori, and the Hokkaido Marathon.
- **[September](/blog/sapporo-in-september):** Autumn Fest, the first autumn colours in Daisetsuzan.
- **[October](/blog/sapporo-in-october):** peak autumn colour, then the golden ginkgo avenue.
- **[November](/blog/sapporo-in-november):** first snow, the White Illumination and the Christmas market.
- **[December](/blog/sapporo-in-december):** Christmas lights, ski season, and snow that stays until spring.

## What to pack

From December to March you need a proper winter coat, gloves, a hat and shoes with grip. The pavements freeze, and the underground walkways between Sapporo Station and Susukino are your friend. In summer a light jacket is enough for the evenings. Spring and autumn swing between warm days and cold nights, so bring layers.
