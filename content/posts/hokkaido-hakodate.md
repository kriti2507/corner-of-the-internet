Hakodate is a historic port city at Hokkaido's southern tip, about 3½ hours from Sapporo on the Hokuto limited express. It deserves at least one night.

## What to do

- **Mt Hakodate at night.** Take the ropeway up after dark for one of Japan's best-known night views: the city lights pinched between two bays.
- **Goryōkaku.** A star-shaped fort from the 1860s. I've been up **Goryōkaku Tower** to see the star shape from above.
- **Motomachi and the bay area.** Western-style churches and old consulates on the hillside, and red-brick warehouses by the water.
- **The morning market.** Squid, crab and rice bowls near the station.
- **Cape Tachimachi.** I've been to this windswept headland at the foot of Mt Hakodate. It's a short walk from the old town.

## The bathing monkeys

At the **Hakodate Tropical Botanical Garden** in Yunokawa onsen town, the Japanese macaques get a pool of hot spring water every winter, from 1 December until Golden Week. They've done it every year since 1971. I've been to the garden.

## On the way

If you drive from Sapporo, **Funkawan Bay Panorama Park** in Yakumo is a good stop. I've stopped there too.

## Good to know

Matsumae, further south, has about 10,000 cherry trees of some 250 varieties that bloom one after another in spring. It makes a good extra day from Hakodate.
