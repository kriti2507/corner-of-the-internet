December is when winter settles in. From late December the snow piles up and stays until spring. Average temperatures sit just below zero.

## What's on

- **Sapporo White Illumination.** The Odori Park lights run until 25 December. Other venues stay lit into the new year.
- **Munich Christmas Market in Sapporo**, until 25 December.
- **Ōkurayama winter fireworks.** Fireworks at the ski jump stadium (2026: 5 December).

## What to do

See the lights, ski on a weekday, and warm up with soup curry or miso ramen. Ōkurayama, the ski jump stadium, has one of the best views over the city.

## Around Hokkaido

- **Asahiyama Zoo's penguin walk** usually starts in late December, once there's enough snow. See [Asahikawa & Daisetsuzan](/blog/hokkaido-asahikawa-daisetsuzan).
- **In Hakodate,** the monkeys at the tropical botanical garden start bathing in hot spring water on 1 December. See [Hakodate](/blog/hokkaido-hakodate).
- **Tomamu's Ice Village,** with an ice bar and an ice chapel, opens in December. See [Furano, Biei & Tomamu](/blog/hokkaido-furano-biei-tomamu).
- **Niseko's powder season** begins. See [Otaru, Shakotan & Niseko](/blog/hokkaido-otaru-shakotan-niseko).

## Good to know

Many places close from 31 December to 3 January. Hotels fill up over New Year, so book early.
