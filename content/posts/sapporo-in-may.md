May is one of the best months in Sapporo. The city turns green, the days get long, and it's warm without being hot. Average highs are around 18°C, and lows around 9°C.

## What's on

- **Cherry blossoms** usually linger into the first week of May, depending on the year.
- **Golden Week** (around 29 April to 5 May). Hitsujigaoka Observation Hill holds sheep shearing and kite flying.
- **Sapporo Lilac Festival.** Sapporo's city tree blooms along Odori Park, with a wine garden that pours more than 200 Hokkaido wines. In 2026 the Odori venue ran from 20 to 31 May, plus a weekend at Kawashimo Park, which has more than 200 kinds of lilac.

## What to do

Get outside. Hike [Sankakuyama](/places/sankakuyama-trail), walk around Maruyama, or sit with a glass of Hokkaido wine at the Lilac Festival. It's also a good month for [coffee](/blog/best-coffee-in-sapporo) on a terrace.

## Around Hokkaido

The Unkai Terrace at Tomamu opens for the season in mid-May (in 2026, from 13 May), for early-morning views over a sea of clouds. See [Furano, Biei & Tomamu](/blog/hokkaido-furano-biei-tomamu). The first flowers start on Rebun Island in late May. See [Wakkanai, Rishiri & Rebun](/blog/hokkaido-wakkanai-rishiri-rebun).

## Good to know

Golden Week is busy and expensive everywhere in Japan. The second half of May is calmer and has better weather.
