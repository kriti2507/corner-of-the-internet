East Hokkaido is far from Sapporo, about 5 hours 20 minutes to Abashiri by train or a long day's drive, but it's the wildest part of Japan. Give it at least three days. Fly into Memanbetsu or Nakashibetsu, and rent a car.

## Shiretoko

A World Heritage peninsula with brown bears, deer and sea eagles. I've been to **Shiretoko National Park** and walked at **Shiretoko Five Lakes**.

At Five Lakes, the elevated boardwalk to the first lake is free and open to everyone. The ground trail around all five lakes has rules because of bears:

- **10 May to 31 July (bear season):** guided tours only.
- **Spring and autumn:** a short paid lecture before you walk.

The park is open from late April to early November. Trails close if a bear is spotted, so check before you go.

## Abashiri and the drift ice

From late January to March, sea ice drifts down from the Amur River and covers the Sea of Okhotsk off Abashiri. The Aurora icebreaker breaks through it on short cruises from Abashiri port. Book ahead in February. I've also been out to **Cape Notoro**, the headland north of the city.

## Akan-Mashū

A cluster of caldera lakes. I camped at **Lake Kussharo**. My note: "Camping place with a better view." I also stopped at the **Bihoro Pass** rest area, which looks over the whole lake. My only note there says: "5 star toilet."

## Good to know

Distances are long, and towns are far apart. Fill up when you can, and don't drive at dusk if you can avoid it, because deer are everywhere.
