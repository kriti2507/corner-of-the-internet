October is autumn colour season in and around Sapporo. It gets cold quickly. By the end of the month, mornings are close to freezing and the high peaks have their first snow.

## What's on

- **Autumn colour at Jōzankei.** The gorge around the hot-spring town usually peaks in early to mid-October. Futami suspension bridge and [Jōzankei Bridge](/places/jozankei-bridge) are the classic viewpoints.
- **The Hokkaido University ginkgo avenue.** About 70 ginkgo trees along a 380 m road turn gold in late October and early November. There's a light-up and a festival on the last weekend of October.
- **Sapporo Marathon** (2026: 4 October) and **Sapporo International Short Film Festival** (2026: 23–25 October).
- **Autumn Fest** runs into the first days of October.

## What to do

Take the bus to Jōzankei for the leaves, walk across the bridges, then soak in an [onsen](/blog/onsen-in-and-around-sapporo). Back in the city, walk through the ginkgo trees at Hokkaido University. [Coffee](/blog/best-coffee-in-sapporo) shops start bringing out their winter menus.

## Around Hokkaido

The nightly fireworks at Lake Tōya run until 31 October. See [Lake Tōya, Noboribetsu & Shikotsu](/blog/hokkaido-lake-toya-noboribetsu-shikotsu). Shiretoko Five Lakes stays open until early November. See [East Hokkaido](/blog/hokkaido-east-shiretoko-abashiri-akan).

## Good to know

Autumn colour comes and goes fast, and the timing changes each year. Check the local foliage reports a week before you go.
