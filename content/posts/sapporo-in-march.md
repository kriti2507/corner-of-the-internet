March is the in-between month. The snow starts to melt, but there's still plenty of it, and spring doesn't really arrive until April. Average highs are around 4–5°C, and lows dip just below zero.

## What's on

- **The White Illumination** stays on at Minami 1-jo, Akapla and the Sapporo Station south plaza until 14 March 2027.
- **Skiing.** The local slopes stay open, and Sapporo Kokusai, up in the mountains, usually runs until early May.

## What to do

With the festival crowds gone, this is a good time for a slow city trip: [coffee](/blog/best-coffee-in-sapporo), [matcha](/blog/matcha-and-tea-in-sapporo), museums, and long [onsen](/blog/onsen-in-and-around-sapporo) evenings. Hotels are cheaper than in February.

## Around Hokkaido

Drift ice usually stays on the Okhotsk coast into March, and in 2026 the Abashiri icebreaker ran until 31 March. See [East Hokkaido](/blog/hokkaido-east-shiretoko-abashiri-akan). The penguin walk at Asahiyama Zoo runs until mid-March. In Hakodate, the monkeys at the tropical botanical garden are still bathing in hot spring water. See [Hakodate](/blog/hokkaido-hakodate).

## Good to know

Melting snow means slush and puddles. Waterproof shoes matter more now than in deep winter. Mornings can still freeze.
