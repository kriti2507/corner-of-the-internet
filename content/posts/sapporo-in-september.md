September is one of the nicest months in Sapporo. The heat goes, the air gets clear, and Hokkaido's harvest season starts. Days are still warm, and evenings are cool.

## What's on

- **Sapporo Autumn Fest.** One of Japan's biggest food festivals, with produce and local dishes from all over Hokkaido at stalls along Odori Park. In 2026 it ran from 11 September to 3 October.
- **Hokkaido Art Fireworks.** Fireworks at Moerenuma Park, the park designed by Isamu Noguchi. In 2026 they were on 5 September.
- **Autumn taste festival at Hitsujigaoka.** Grilled saury and scallops at the observation hill in late September.

## What to do

Eat your way along Odori at Autumn Fest, then walk it off around Maruyama or up [Sankakuyama](/places/sankakuyama-trail). It's a good month for a road trip, with fewer crowds than in summer.

## Around Hokkaido

The mountains of Daisetsuzan are usually the first place in Japan where the autumn colours turn, from mid-September. See [Asahikawa & Daisetsuzan](/blog/hokkaido-asahikawa-daisetsuzan). The Unkai Terrace at Tomamu stays open into mid-October. See [Furano, Biei & Tomamu](/blog/hokkaido-furano-biei-tomamu).

## Good to know

There are a few public holidays in late September (Silver Week), and some years they make a long weekend. Check the calendar before you book.
