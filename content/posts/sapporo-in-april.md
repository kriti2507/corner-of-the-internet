April is when spring finally reaches Sapporo. The snow in the city is gone by early or mid-April. Average highs climb to around 12°C, but nights are still cold, around 3°C.

## What's on

- **Jōzankei carp streamers.** About 400 koinobori fly over the Toyohira River in the hot-spring town, from early April into early May. 2026 was the 40th year.
- **Cherry blossoms.** In Hokkaido they bloom later than in the rest of Japan, usually in late April and early May. Maruyama Park and Hokkaido Shrine are the classic spots. In warm years they come earlier: in 2026 full bloom in Sapporo was forecast for around 20 April, more than ten days early.
- **Hiraoka plum blossoms.** The plum grove at Hiraoka Park blooms from late April to early May, often alongside the cherries.
- **Golden Week** starts at the end of April. It's one of Japan's busiest travel weeks, so book ahead.

## What to do

Walk. After months of snow, the parks fill up the moment the ground is dry. In Hokkaido, people often have a jingisukan (lamb barbecue) under the cherry trees. Early April, before the blossoms, is one of the cheapest and quietest times to visit.

## Around Hokkaido

The fireworks over Lake Tōya start again at the end of April and run every night until October. See [Lake Tōya, Noboribetsu & Shikotsu](/blog/hokkaido-lake-toya-noboribetsu-shikotsu). The monkeys in Hakodate keep bathing until Golden Week. See [Hakodate](/blog/hokkaido-hakodate).

## Good to know

Weather swings a lot this month. Bring a warm jacket even if the forecast says sunny.
